Approach

The Weave: one workflow at a time

Four steps, each with a clear deliverable. You decide at the end of every step whether to continue.

The four steps

  1. 1 · Discover

    Pick one workflow

    We map it with your team and agree the quality bar and the cost limit.

    You getA one-page plan: scope, measures, price.

  2. 2 · Prove

    Pilot on real data

    A working pilot on your real data, measured against the bar.

    You getA demo and a results report.

  3. 3 · Weave in

    Connect and hand over

    Connected to your systems, security-reviewed, and your team trained.

    You getA live agent in your tools, and a runbook.

  4. 4 · Run

    Measure and improve

    We monitor quality and cost and improve it every month.

    You getA monthly report, fixes and upgrades.

Every engagement includes

  • 01A quality bar, agreed in writing before the pilot.
  • 02A cost limit per task, monitored.
  • 03Your data stays yours. Where possible it runs in your cloud, and it is never used to train models.
  • 04You own the code, prompts and evaluations we build for you.

As set out in your signed agreement. Third-party models and tools stay under their own licences.

How pricing works

  • Discover: a fixed fee, agreed up front.
  • Prove: a fixed scope and a fixed fee.
  • Run: a monthly fee for monitoring and improvement.

Indian clients are quoted in INR plus GST. International clients are quoted in USD.

Questions buyers ask

Who owns what you build?

You do. The code, prompts and evaluations we build for you are yours, as set out in your signed agreement. Third-party models and tools stay under their own licences.

Where does our data go?

Where possible, the system runs in your own cloud account. If it can't, we use named providers in a region you choose, and list them in the agreement.

Do you train models on our data?

No. We only use model providers whose enterprise terms exclude training on client data.

Can we switch models or providers later?

Yes. We design systems so the model can be changed, and the evaluations show whether a new model is as good as the old one.

What happens if the pilot misses the quality bar?

We agree the bar, and what happens if it's missed, before the pilot starts, and write both into the agreement.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Indian clients are quoted in INR plus GST; international clients in USD.
