How an engagement runs

The Weave

Our approach
  1. 1 · Discover

    Pick one workflow

    We map it with your team and agree the quality bar and the cost limit.

    You getA one-page plan: scope, measures, price.

  2. 2 · Prove

    Pilot on real data

    A working pilot on your real data, measured against the bar.

    You getA demo and a results report.

  3. 3 · Weave in

    Connect and hand over

    Connected to your systems, security-reviewed, and your team trained.

    You getA live agent in your tools, and a runbook.

  4. 4 · Run

    Measure and improve

    We monitor quality and cost and improve it every month.

    You getA monthly report, fixes and upgrades.
