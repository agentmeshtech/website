Services

What we build

Six kinds of AI systems, each built around one of your workflows and measured against a quality bar and a cost limit agreed with you.

01

Workflow agents

Agents that carry out multi-step tasks inside your tools: read the request, look things up, take the next step, and hand over to a person when approval is needed.

Typical tasks
  • Triage incoming requests and route them
  • Reconcile records between two systems
  • Follow up on open items until they close
What we measure

Share of tasks completed without edits; time per task; cost per task.

02

Knowledge assistants

Answers from your own documents, policies and past tickets, with the source shown for every answer.

Typical tasks
  • HR and policy questions
  • Product and process questions for support teams
  • Onboarding help for new staff
What we measure

Accuracy on a test set of real questions; share of answers with a correct source; share escalated to a person.

03

Document AI

Reads invoices, contracts, forms and scans, pulls out the fields you need, checks them against your rules and files the result.

Typical tasks
  • Invoice and receipt capture
  • Onboarding and KYC forms
  • Contract data extraction
What we measure

Field-level accuracy; share needing human correction; documents per hour.

04

Ask your data

Lets people ask questions of your databases and spreadsheets in plain language, and answers with a table or chart plus the query used.

Typical tasks
  • Sales and collections questions
  • Stock and order status
  • Management summaries
What we measure

Correct answers on a question bank; time to answer; cost per question.

05

AI inside your systems

Connects AI to the software you already run, such as ERP, CRM, HRMS, ticketing, email and WhatsApp, with the right access controls.

Typical tasks
  • Draft replies inside your helpdesk
  • Update CRM records from emails and calls
  • Push approved entries into accounting
What we measure

Adoption by the team; errors caught before they reach the system; availability.

06

Quality & cost monitoring

Test sets, dashboards and alerts that keep AI accurate and within budget, including systems we did not build.

Typical tasks
  • Evaluation suites before each release
  • Cost and speed tracking per task
  • Alerts when quality drops
What we measure

Quality score over time; cost per task against the agreed limit; issues caught.
