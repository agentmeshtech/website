Examples

What we'd build for you

Six engagements we offer, each with what the agent does and how we'd know it's working.

Illustrations of engagements we offer, not past client work.

ExampleFinance

Invoice-to-books agent

Reads supplier invoices from email, matches them to purchase orders and drafts entries for approval.

What we'd measure
% matched without edits; minutes per invoice.
ExampleHR

Policy desk

Answers employee HR questions on chat, citing the policy page.

What we'd measure
Accuracy on a test set; % escalated.
ExampleLegal

Contract checker

Compares a contract with your standard terms and lists the deviations by risk.

What we'd measure
Deviations caught compared with a lawyer's review.
ExampleCustomer support

Support copilot

Drafts replies from help docs and routes hard tickets to people.

What we'd measure
First-reply time; % of drafts accepted.
ExampleManagement

Ask-your-data

Answers plain-language questions over sales data with a table and a chart.

What we'd measure
Correct answers on a question bank; cost per question.
ExampleOperations

PO chaser

Tracks open purchase orders, emails suppliers and updates the tracker.

What we'd measure
Late orders caught; hours saved per week.
